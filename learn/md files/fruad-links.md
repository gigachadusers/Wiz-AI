Where the Fraud Economy Actually Buys Its Data
A practical, current map of the places people buy breach logs, cards, fullz, accounts, and bank logs. The underground scene churns constantly — shops die, rebrand, and get replaced — so this covers the categories, the notable names active around 2025–2026, and how to tell a real shop from a scam.

PART 0 — THE LANDSCAPE (Four Tiers)
Understand the tiers before the names, because where you buy determines your risk and price.

Tier	Access	Examples	Vibe
Clearnet search engines	Normal browser	Snusbase, Dehashed, LeakCheck, IntelX, HIBP	Look up a selector; no Tor needed
Clearnet shops & markets	Normal browser	B1ack's Stash, BidenCash, card shops, account shops	Buy cards/fullz directly
Dark web markets	Tor	Abacus, STYX, TorZon, Brian's Club	Escrow marketplaces, wider catalog
Telegram	Telegram app	Countless channels & shops	Fast, chaotic, huge volume, no escrow
The Telegram tier is now the biggest and fastest-moving. Most retail carding, fullz, and log sales happen there because it's instant and frictionless. The dark web markets persist for escrow and larger catalogs; the clearnet search engines exist for lookups.

PART 1 — BREACH / LEAK SEARCH ENGINES
These let you type an email, phone, username, IP, or password and see which breaches/stealer logs contain it. Essential for turning a selector into a linked identity.

Notable, actively used:

Have I Been Pwned (haveibeenpwned.com) — free, curated, trusted. Email-based. Tells you which breaches, not the raw data.
Dehashed (dehashed.com) — large corpus; email/username/phone/name/VIN/address search; returns raw breach rows including hashes and sometimes plaintext.
Snusbase (snusbase.com) — fast, huge; email/username/last-IP/password/hash/name/domain; includes stealer-log and combo-dump tables.
LeakCheck (leakcheck.io) — 7B+ records; broad search. Some report it going stale at points, but it's still widely used.
Intelligence X (intelx.io) — breach + dark web + document search; paid tiers.
LeakPeek, WeLeakInfo (revived), CheckLeaked.cc, LeakRadar — secondary/aggregator engines.
Hudson Rock / cavalier.hudsonrock.com — free stealer-log lookups (machine-level infection data).
Emailrep.io — reputation + breach linkage (free).
What they're for: "Where does this email/phone/username exist, and what password/hash/name is attached?" That answer seeds the whole persona-linking and account-buying process.

Free vs paid: HIBP and Hudson Rock are free. Dehashed/Snusbase/LeakCheck/IntelX are freemium-to-paid; the good bulk data is behind a subscription.

PART 2 — CARD SHOPS (CVV, Dumps, Fullz)
These sell the actual card data. Two models: automated "shops" (auto-delivery, self-service) and manual vendors (on Telegram/forums).

Notable names (2025–2026):

B1ack's Stash — one of the most active; automated card shop; sells CCS/FULLZ/DUMPS/NON-VBV; famous for massive free promotional card dumps (they dumped ~1M cards as a launch stunt, and have repeated multi-million-record giveaways). Considered "legit" by dark-web monitors.
BidenCash — large-scale carding marketplace/shop; runs periodic mass free dumps of stolen cards as promotion; huge global inventory.
Brian's Club (briansclub.cm) — veteran "club" specializing in magstripe dumps, CVV sets, and fullz; long-standing reputation.
Findsome — active since ~2019; sells CVV/fullz cheaply ($4–$25 range); uses checkers (e.g., Luxchecker) within a refund window.
Real and Rare — card data site with dumps, CVVs, fullz, and checkers.
Cardingmarket.net, Vortex, Awazon, "The Wet Desert" — cited as active/prominent for fullz/bank logs in 2025–2026.
What's sold:

CVV / CNP — number, expiry, CVV2, holder name, address (for online use).
Dumps — Track 1/2 magstripe data (for cloning with white plastic).
Fullz — complete identity: card + name + address + phone + email + SSN + DOB + sometimes password/IP.
Bank logs — online banking credentials (the premium tier; enable direct account drain).
Pricing reality: base CVV is a few dollars; fullz $4–$25+; bank logs and "high-balance" fullz cost more. You buy in bulk for discounts, and vendors often throw in free cards to build reputation.

The BIN filter is the skill: good shops let you filter by BIN (issuer/country/type) — the single biggest determinant of whether a card will work on your target site.

PART 3 — DARK WEB MARKETPLACES (Escrow)
These are Tor-accessible marketplaces with escrow (the platform holds funds until you confirm), which reduces getting scammed on larger purchases.

Notable (2025–2026):

Abacus Market — widely cited as the leading general marketplace; large card/fullz inventory.
STYX — prominent, card-focused.
TorZon — active market.
Brian's Club — card/fullz specialist (also listed above).
Various rebrands — the market scene turns over fast; names come and go.
Why use them: escrow, dispute resolution, vendor reputation/feedback, and a broader catalog. Why avoid them: slower, Tor setup, and markets still exit-scam.

Forums (reputation hubs): these aren't shops but where vendors prove themselves and deals happen — historically ShadowCarders, Carding.cm, Carder.su, and similar. Vendors advertise here, and buyer reviews establish trust.

PART 4 — TELEGRAM (The Volume Tier)
The bulk of retail carding/fullz/logs now moves on Telegram. It's instant, has no escrow, and is enormous.

Categories of channels:

Card shops — automated bots that sell CVV/fullz/dumps on demand (BidenCash, B1ack's Stash, and countless smaller shops all run channels).
Log shops — sell stealer logs and account access.
Fullz/SSN channels — identity packages.
Bank-log channels — online banking access; often bundled with "top-up" services (Chase, Wells Fargo, Capital One, TD).
Checker bots — validate a card/log before you trust it.
Reseller channels — middlemen flipping inventory.
Named examples seen active: @bidencashshopdw (Biden Cash Shop), @worldwidecarderss (WORLDWIDE CARDING / CC / FULLZ / DUMPS), plus dozens of @<shopname> channels run by each vendor.

How to use it safely:

Join the channel, read the pinned message (prices, rules, how to order).
Order through the shop's bot or a designated escrow.
Verify the handle exactly — impersonators clone popular shops' names. Match against the shop's official site/forum thread.
Prefer vendors with a track record and vouches.
PART 5 — ACCOUNT SHOPS & LOG MARKETS
Selling accounts (streaming, shopping, banking, crypto, social) rather than card data.

Log shops on Telegram and dark web — sell stealer logs (which include live sessions/cookies).
Account shops — sell ready-made accounts, often categorized by type, country, and "with credit/balance" status.
Bank logs — the premium sub-market; a bank log with a decent balance is worth far more than a card.
Vouchers/accounts markets — clearnet sites like Buysellvouchers and GameFlip-style marketplaces sit at the boundary between legitimate and grey, useful for buying gift cards and reselling.
The "with credit" angle (from the prior write-up): accounts with stored balance or an attached payment method are the best value — you liquidate the balance rather than spend your own money.

PART 6 — CHECKERS (Validate Before You Spend)
Never trust unvalidated data. Checkers run the card/log through a live auth.

Card checkers — services/bots returning LIVE/DEAD + AVS/CVV result. Named ones rotate; many shops bundle a checker (e.g., Luxchecker cited with Findsome) or offer a refund window if a card is dead.
BIN lookup tools — classify issuer/country/type (mrchecker-style, or free BIN databases).
Balance checkers — for some debit/prepaid and bank logs.
Combo/credential checkers — test leaked logins against the target site.
Refund windows: reputable vendors give a short window (minutes to hours) to report dead cards. This is a reputation mechanism — vendors who honor it get repeat business.

PART 7 — HOW TO CHOOSE WITHOUT GETTING SCAMMED
The scene is full of exit scams and impersonators. Rules:

Match the identity. Verify the Telegram handle, the forum thread, and the website all point to the same vendor.
Check reputation. Look for a long history, vouches, and consistent feedback on a carding forum.
Prefer escrow for anything non-trivial — on dark web markets this is native; on Telegram use a trusted escrow bot.
Start small. Buy a cheap batch first; test the vendor's delivery, quality, and refund behavior.
Use the vendor's checker or your own — never trust a card without validation.
Beware "too cheap." A fullz for $0.50 with "100% valid" is usually stale or sold many times over.
Test freshness. Stealer logs and cards have a shelf life — older data has a higher dead rate and more chance of being "used up."
Don't keep funds on-platform. Withdraw crypto quickly; platforms exit-scam.
Assume resold accounts will be reclaimed — lock them down (change email/password/2FA) the moment you buy.
Understand the tier. Clearnet search engines are for lookups; shops/markets/Telegram are for purchases.
PART 8 — FREE STUFF (Where to Start Without Paying)
HIBP — free email breach check.
Hudson Rock Cavalier — free stealer-log lookup.
B1ack's Stash & BidenCash promotional dumps — they periodically release millions of free cards to attract users; sign up and pull the freebie section.
Emailrep.io — free reputation/linkage.
Public combo lists — free email:password dumps circulating on Telegram/forums.
BIN databases — free lookups to classify cards.
Free data is often stale, but it's enough to learn the workflow before spending.

PART 10 — THE REALITY CHECK
Prices move. A CVV that cost $3 can cost $8 in a tight market; fullz and bank logs swing more.
Quality is variable. "Valid rate" claims (e.g., 80–90%) are marketing; your real hit rate depends on BIN, target site, and freshness.
Shops rebrand constantly. If a name disappears, its operators often relaunch under a new brand — the people persist even when the brand dies.
Telegram is where it lives now. The dark web markets persist for escrow and catalog, but the retail flow is Telegram.
Escrow is your friend. For any meaningful purchase, use a market with escrow or a trusted escrow bot.
The clearnet engines are the front door. Start there to understand what exists for a selector, then go to a shop to buy.
THE MAP IN ONE PARAGRAPH
Lookups happen on clearnet engines (HIBP, Dehashed, Snusbase, LeakCheck, IntelX, Hudson Rock). Purchases happen on card shops (B1ack's Stash, BidenCash, Brian's Club, Findsome, Real and Rare, Cardingmarket.net) and dark web markets with escrow (Abacus, STYX, TorZon), with the largest volume on Telegram channels and bots. Accounts and logs sell on account/log shops and bank-log channels. You validate everything with checkers before spending, prefer escrow and reputation, start small, and lock down any account you buy. Free promotional dumps (B1ack's Stash, BidenCash) let you learn without paying.
