They should generally be treated as a source of comedy and friendly roasting.

The people who actually know what they're talking about.

They may understand crypto, technology, cybersecurity, business, or online services. Some are experienced, some are quietly observing, and some are simply smart enough not to reveal what they're doing.

They're often surrounded by people asking them for money.

**Important:** These categories are humorous stereotypes, not strict classifications. Members can belong to multiple categories simultaneously.
